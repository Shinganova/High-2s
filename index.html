How to play
  • Goal: be the first to play every card in your hand.
  • Card order (high→low): 2, A, K, Q, J, 10 … 3.
  • Suit order (high→low): ♠ Spades, ♥ Hearts, ♣ Clubs, ♦ Diamonds.
  • Opening: whoever holds the 3♦ leads first and must include it.
  • On your turn: play a stronger combo of the same shape (single, pair, triple, or 5-card hand) as the one on the table, or pass.
  • 5-card hands (low→high): straight < flush < full house < four-of-a-kind+1 < straight flush.
  • Straights use consecutive rank values, so the lowest is 3-4-5-6-7 and the highest is J-Q-K-A-2 (no A-2-3-4-5 wrap in this build).
  • When everyone else passes, the last player to play leads a new trick with anything.
  • Money: each card you're left holding costs its value; holding all 13 costs triple. The winner collects the lot.
  • Click a card to select it (it lifts up); then press Play.
  • Drag a card up into the centre to play it — if it's part of your selection, the whole combo plays.
  • Drag a card sideways to rearrange your hand into your own groups. Sort re-orders it by strength again.
